Privacy & limitations

TA7 Money Tracker stores data in your browser's local storage; it doesn't send your financial records to a server. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to see them. Browser storage can be cleared, and private browsing may not retain data. Use backups. This prototype does not connect to banks or automatically import payments.
